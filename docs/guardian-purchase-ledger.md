## State rules

- **Reference:** listing or category found; stock, dimensions and seller must be rechecked.
- **Selected:** exact SKU passes fit, electrical and connector checks.
- **Purchased:** order evidence exists.
- **Bench verified:** the received unit passes the USB test.
- **Field verified:** the integrated shield passes human-use and safety checks.

Do not bulk-buy six boards from the first listing. Buy one controller and one sensor for the bench proof, then lock the SKU only after it passes. Marketplace links intentionally open a narrow search rather than pretending one seller is permanently safe: compare seller history, local delivery, return policy and the exact selected variant on the day of purchase.
